Jan 2010 – Apr 2018
Dashed line: ordinary least-squares linear regression. The model fits January 2010–December 2017 to avoid confusing partial-year 2018 with a full year. The extension is descriptive, not a validated operational forecast. Actual observations stop in April 2018; absent subsequent months are not plotted as zeros.
